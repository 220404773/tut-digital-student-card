Academic prototype notice. This system is a student project built for academic assessment at Tshwane University of Technology. It is not an official TUT service, and a card issued by it is not an official University identity document. These terms have not been reviewed by a legal practitioner.

Last updated: 28 August 2026

1. Agreement to these terms

By creating an account or using this system, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with them, please do not create an account.

2. What this system does

The system lets a registered student upload a proof of registration, reads it automatically, and issues a digital student card with a QR code that a third party can scan to check whether the card is currently valid.

3. Eligibility

You may only create an account if you are a registered student and you are using your own identity and your own proof of registration. One account is permitted per student number and per email address.

4. Your responsibilities

You agree that you will:

  • Provide accurate information and upload only your own genuine proof of registration.
  • Keep your password confidential and not share your account with anyone.
  • Tell the operator promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.
  • Keep your details up to date on your profile page.

You agree that you will not:

  • Upload a forged, altered or another person's document, or attempt to obtain a card you are not entitled to.
  • Present a card, screenshot or QR code as if it were an official University identity document.
  • Attempt to bypass the verification, rate limiting, or access controls of the system, or access another user's account or records.
  • Copy, scrape, disrupt, overload or reverse engineer the system, or use automated tools against it without permission.

5. Verification is automated

Cards are issued automatically based on software reading your document. That reading can be wrong. A document is only auto-approved when the system is sufficiently confident; otherwise it is referred for manual review. The issuing of a card is not a guarantee that your registration status is correct, and the operator may correct, revoke or refuse a card where a mistake or misuse is found.

6. Card validity, expiry and revocation

  • Your card's expiry date is taken from the registration period on the document you uploaded.
  • A card automatically becomes expired once that date has passed, and it will then fail verification.
  • You can reactivate an expired card yourself by uploading a current proof of registration.
  • An administrator may revoke a card at any time, for example where misuse is suspected. A revoked card fails verification.
  • A document whose registration period has already ended, or whose end date is implausibly far in the future, will be rejected and no card will be issued.

If your account is deleted, your card and its history are permanently removed; a record of the deletion is retained for audit purposes as described in the Privacy Policy. You may request deletion of your own account at any time.
